GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Does Stream24 host my video processing?

The current local-worker edition runs FFmpeg on your own connected PC. Your media paths stay on that worker.

Can I schedule streams?

Yes. Configure daily or weekly schedules with a timezone from the dashboard.

Can I connect more than one worker?

Yes. Add workers to your workspace and configure backup-worker failover where your media is synchronized and preflight-ready.
